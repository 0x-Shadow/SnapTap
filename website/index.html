01 — Why SnapTap

Built for people who
screenshot fifty times a day.

Capture → paste. Nothing between.

Every screenshot lands on your clipboard the instant it's taken. No editor opening, no extra clicks, no hunting for the file.

A gallery that respects you

Everything auto-saves to Pictures/SnapTap/. Click any thumbnail to enlarge it, copy it again, or delete it.

Pixel-perfect when it counts

A tray toggle switches captures to native display resolution. Crisp text in docs, bug reports, and design handoffs.

Speaks multi-monitor

Captures the display under your cursor by default — or lock to one monitor from the tray. Unplugging a screen never breaks it.

Spam-proofed

Throttled captures, a reused preview window, tiny IPC payloads, and auto-recovery if a renderer ever dies. Mash the button — it holds.

Yours, fully

MIT-licensed, zero network calls, zero telemetry. Your screenshots never leave your disk. Audit the code on GitHub.
