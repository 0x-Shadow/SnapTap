Why SnapTap

Made for people who screenshot fifty times a day.

Capture. Paste. Done.

Every screenshot lands on your clipboard the instant it's taken. No editor popping open, no extra clicks, no hunting for the file.

A gallery that respects you.

Everything auto-saves to Pictures/SnapTap. Click any thumbnail to enlarge it, copy it again, or delete it.

Pixel-perfect when it counts.

A tray toggle switches captures to native display resolution. Crisp text in docs, bug reports, and handoffs.

Speaks multi-monitor.

Captures the display under your cursor — or lock to one monitor from the tray. Unplugging a screen never breaks it.

Spam-proofed.

Throttled captures, a reused preview window, tiny IPC payloads, auto-recovery if a renderer dies. Mash the button — it holds.

Yours, fully.

MIT-licensed, zero network calls, zero telemetry. Audit every line on GitHub.
